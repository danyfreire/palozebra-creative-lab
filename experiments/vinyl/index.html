REC IN PLACE

Arm REC, touch the platter, scratch, stop. Rewind the DAW and the gesture returns at the same point.

HOW IT WORKS

Vinyl is a non-destructive insert. Your original audio or MIDI remains untouched; bypass the plug-in and the clean source is still there.

01Put Palozebra Vinyl on the track you want to scratch.
02Press Play in your DAW, then REC in Vinyl.
03Touch the record at the musical moment you want and perform the scratch.
04Press STOP REC. Rewind and play normally: the scratch reappears in place.
05When it is right, print it with your DAW's normal render, bounce or resample workflow.
